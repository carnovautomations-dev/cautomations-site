Privacy policy.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are

cautomations is the trading name of Antonio Carnovale, a sole trader based in Turin, Italy, who builds n8n and AI automations for businesses. Antonio Carnovale is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: ty@cautomations.com

What data we collect

  • When you contact us (email, booking a call, messages on freelance platforms): your name, email address, company and the content of your message.
  • When you visit this website: technical data such as IP address and browser type, processed by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) to deliver and protect the site. We do not use advertising or profiling cookies.
  • When we build automations for a client: the business data the client chooses to connect (for example leads, contacts, calendar events or call transcripts). For this data we act as a processor on behalf of the client, who remains the controller.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your requests and send quotes (pre-contractual steps).
  • To deliver, maintain and support the automations agreed with our clients (performance of a contract).
  • To keep invoices and records required by Italian tax law (legal obligation).
  • To keep our systems secure (legitimate interest).

We never sell personal data and never use it for advertising.

Google user data

Our automation platform can connect to Google services (such as Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Calendar) through Google OAuth, only after the account owner grants access.

  • We access Google user data only to run the automations the account owner has set up, for example writing a new lead to a spreadsheet or creating a calendar booking.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, except the service providers needed to run the automation, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
  • The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

Our automation server runs on Hetzner infrastructure in the European Union. Depending on the automation, data may also be processed by the tools a client chooses to use (for example Google, OpenAI, Vapi or Telegram), under their own terms. Where these providers process data outside the EU, transfers rely on the safeguards provided by those providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Contact requests: up to 24 months after the last exchange.
  • Client automation data: for the duration of the contract, then deleted or returned to the client.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 10 years, as required by Italian law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to ty@cautomations.com and we will reply within 30 days.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
