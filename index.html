Lead system · window & door installers

Every lead answered, none forgotten.

Requests from the website and from retail stands used to sit in an inbox or a paper notebook. Now each lead lands in a Telegram thread for that client, with buttons to update its status, automatic follow-up emails and a morning summary of what is still open.

Leads can also be added by voice note straight from the job site. Everything is stored in Postgres on my own server, with nightly backups.

Miniad · site-survey tool

The quote is ready before you leave the house.

A tablet app for the site survey. The installer enters every window and shutter as he measures, picks from a price list of 18 product types, and hands the customer a finished quote at the end of the visit. The deposit gets taken on the spot instead of two weeks later.
